# Astra review round 2

1. **Dot scope: accept Fable.**
The owner should not have to visit three screens to discover a decision.
Count deduplicated attention items, Proposals, actionable drafts and required sign-ins, never transient coverage failures.
Unknown or truncated inputs cannot establish an exact zero.
2. **Headings: accept Fable.**
The live count earns the first line; slogans cost the owner visible work on every visit.
Other screens use their plain names, and the briefing has its own timestamp.
3. **Type: compromise.**
Accept bundled faces within a measured combined 150 KB budget, with system sans and Georgia as fallback.
Linux substitution is a real objection, but the prototype deliberately retains the fallback until actual licensed font files and sizes are measured.
4. **Density: accept Fable.**
Use 14.5 px interface text and 32 px desktop buttons, retaining 16 px reading text and 44 px controls on coarse pointers or narrow screens.
Long titles wrap when expanded; compact must not mean inaccessible.
5. **Colour: accept Fable.**
Copper means brand, selection or action, never running, unverified or healthy status.
Brass qualifies attention and coverage; red marks failures with explanatory words.
6. **Inbox filter: compromise.**
Use wrapping count links to actual group headings and a separate Hidden toggle.
These are navigation controls, contrary to Fable's claim that they are not controls, so keep their order and focused link stable during updates.
7. **Replication: accept Fable.**
One Machines and Replication section in Settings is enough, with Inbox failure items and Reader context linking to it.

## Remaining objections

Do not promise that every automatic save has one-click Revert.
Events and note-text appends are explicitly excluded, and later edits can invalidate eligibility.
Do not call route defaults hard caps or turn a capped component count into a deduplicated total without proving the bound.
Keep mutation shortcuts off by default, including Done and Dismiss, because being reversible does not make accidental queue changes helpful.
The v2 narrow screenshot spends almost its entire first screen before the second item; collapse the dated briefing on narrow screens.
Avoid automatically selecting the newest failed job on every update, because that breaks the shared rule of preserving the owner's place.

Read Fable's v2 design, prototype, review and desktop and narrow Inbox screenshots.
Fable's round 2 spec and review were not present at the time of this pass; they remain the round 3 review input.
