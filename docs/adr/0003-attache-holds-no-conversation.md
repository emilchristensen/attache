# Attache holds no conversation of its own

Attache shipped with a chat box that answered questions from the Brain, plus voice dictation and spoken replies around it.
We remove all of it and make Attache a Dashboard: it shows the Brain and the inbox and takes one-click decisions, and every question to the Brain or addition to it happens in the owner's Harness.
The owner already works in a Harness all day, and a second, weaker conversation inside Attache split attention and duplicated what the Harness does better.

## Consequences

- The Dashboard does not capture, edit or correct notes.
  It approves or rejects proposals, dismisses or snoozes inbox items, approves drafts, and sets up connections and Replication.
- Attache has to be reachable from every Harness the owner uses, including ones without a shell.
- Recall quality is now measured by what a Harness gets back from a search, not by a written answer from Attache.
- Do not add a chat box back to the Dashboard without the owner deciding it again.
